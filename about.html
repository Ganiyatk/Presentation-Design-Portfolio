My Design Philosophy

Good design communicates. Before I touch a single slide, I ask: who is this for, what do they need to understand, and what should they do after seeing it?

From there, everything is intentional. I simplify where needed, structure information so it's easy to follow, and remove anything that competes with the message. The result is a presentation that doesn't just support the speaker — it does part of the work for them.

Because at the end of the day, a deck that looks beautiful but confuses the room has failed its job.

My Design Process

How a project moves from brief to boardroom

01

Discovery

We align on your goals, audience, and the message your presentation needs to deliver. You share your content, references, and expectations.

02

Structure

Before anything gets designed, I organize your content, simplify the narrative, and map out the flow. This is where clarity is built.

03

Design

Your content comes to life. Layouts, typography, colour, data visualisation as needed and on-brand.

04

Finalize

You review, I refine. Once we're aligned on every detail, you receive a presentation that's ready to be shared, pitched, or presented with confidence.
